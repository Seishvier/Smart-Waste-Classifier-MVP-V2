    Bin Color Guide

    Green – Organic

    Food scraps, leaves, garden waste. Compostable materials only.

    Blue – Paper & Cardboard

    Newspapers, magazines, clean cardboard boxes. Keep dry.

    Yellow – Plastic, Metal & Glass

    Bottles, cans, jars. Rinse first. Check local rules for mixed plastics.

    Red – Hazardous (B3)

    Batteries, chemicals, electronics, medical waste. Never mix with regular trash.

    Grey – Residual

    Non-recyclable mixed waste, heavily soiled items, composite materials.

    Helpful Articles

    What is composite waste?

    Items made of multiple materials that are hard to separate (e.g. juice boxes, laminated packaging).

    How to rinse packaging properly

    A quick rinse with cold water is usually enough. No need for soap. Dry before recycling.

    Why batteries need special care

    They can leak toxic chemicals or cause fires. Always take them to designated collection points.
